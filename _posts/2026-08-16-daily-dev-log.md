## Today's Update

This was a consolidation day — not in the sense of tying loose ends, but in the sense that several large bodies of work that have been running in parallel finally reached a point where they could be declared complete and archived. The compositional market analysis feature, the architecture review remediation, and the Shopify OAuth onboarding all crossed their finish lines today, and the bulk of the day's energy went into making sure those closures were clean rather than just technically done.

The compositional market analysis work is the one worth describing in some depth because of its scale. The full implementation ran from tenant storage migration through eight numbered phases: MI tenant cutover (adding Billing Account columns across 21 models, building the backfill task, enforcing consistency constraints, and retiring the `BelongsToMiTenant` trait), taxonomy and schema foundation (nine closed-enum taxonomies covering analytical levels, evidence confidence, capability maturity, and market relationships, plus migrations for Market Definitions, Vertical Families, Propositions, Claim Graphs, Evidence families, and subject snapshots), the recomposition engine (the run Action and worker job, idempotency and retry tests, the five workspace panels for contract, claim graph, evidence, run status, and pinned results), market envelope and coverage (requirement graph validator, MCPE closure, jurisdictional overlay review, coverage evaluator, readiness blocker, gap derivation, and MVC profile), the competitive intelligence layer (competitor and substitute stack schemas, all the profile evaluators from cohesion through attractiveness through opportunity assessment), governed publication (contract activation, planning candidate release, five report renderers, disclosure/redaction evaluation, and the full publication lifecycle with expiry, withdrawal, and supersession), capability combination discovery (combination detection, adjacent market matching, Vertical Family versioning and reanalysis impact, portfolio projection), and finally the continuous intelligence layer (scheduled intelligence ingestion, materiality policy, reanalysis candidate creation, run comparison, competitive trajectory, differentiation decay, and legacy finding import). Each phase had cross-account denial tests, policy tests, and immutability tests layered throughout. The final phase today was documentation, quality gates, security review, performance profiling, and acceptance matrix reconciliation — then archiving the tracker. That's genuinely the full scope of a market analysis product from schema to publication lifecycle.

The architecture review remediation closed in parallel. That work addressed eight domains: COPPA age classification (migrating from raw age gates to Age Status-only persistence, then deriving COPPA compatibility state from that single source), idempotency (canonical request digest reservation), settlement (idempotent orchestration Action, thin controller boundary, removing the duplicate `Tenancy/IdempotencyKey` model path), Experience artifact lifecycle (introducing the lifecycle enum, casting all lifecycle-bearing models, locking and atomically transitioning versions, replacing string comparisons throughout), consent (the full immutable ledger — 11 separate migrations, the digest-bound append, current posture, evidence attachment, expiry, withdrawal conflict preservation, bulk operation isolation, and removal of all duplicate writers), subscription cancellation governance, Contact merge and deletion (normalized persistence, locked tenant-safe merge resolution, blocking canonical Contact deletion when merged dependents exist), and Registry activation compatibility. Each slice followed the same red-green pattern: failing tests first, implementation, quality gates. I'll admit the consent slice was the most involved — the interaction between posture authority, withdrawal conflicts, and idempotent expiry sweeps required more test fixture precision than I initially estimated. The `consent-harden-ledger-test-fixtures` item at the end of that sequence wasn't planned as a separate work unit when I started the slice; it emerged from the test coverage revealing fixture assumptions that didn't hold under concurrent replay.

The Shopify OAuth onboarding for existing stores is complete: architecture boundary, platform-owned configuration, the OAuth provider boundary, tenant-bound start and callback actions, idempotent store persistence and reconnection, first-sync with durable retry semantics, the one-field connect UI replacing the old secret-entry setup, security tests covering OAuth rejection and tenant isolation, and retry tests covering reconnect and first-sync failure recovery. The two synthetic estate items — the versioned manifest/oracle/mutation matrix and the idempotent GraphQL estate builder with ledger and verifier — are also done and feed directly into the harness work. The PHPStan ratchet remediation covered 21 items: knowledge cutoff and jurisdiction handling, MCP domain-transfer idempotency (spike, implement, verify), `BelongsToAccount` late-static builder generics, principal key narrowing, audit deduplication value narrowing, removing instance access from the static tenant route closure, and final ledger reconciliation.

The remaining items today were a collection of correctness fixes and structural improvements across the platform: serializing contact deletion with active merges in the CDP layer, aligning merge coherence constraints, preflighting merge constraints before the merge operation runs, adding MariaDB check constraint support to the migrations layer, requiring authoritative age status in COPPA, normalizing cancellation replay time and backfilling cancellation pairs in billing, publishing governed mutation contracts through the API, excluding merged contacts from marketing metrics, binding native MCP calls to the current projection, and validating native projection hashes. Several of these — particularly the merge serialization and the constraint preflighting — exist because the Contact domain now has a governed merge resolution path, and the surrounding infrastructure needs to respect the invariants that path establishes. The August briefing reconciliations also got published today, which closes out the strategy documentation thread that's been sitting open since last week.

With the CMA feature and architecture remediation both archived, the planning index is significantly cleaner. What's left visible is the Shopify ETL pipeline, the consent and CDP work that the remediation opened up, and the MCP boundary. The consent work in particular — given how much of the remediation touched the ledger layer — probably warrants a pass through the full integration path before anything new is layered on top.
